Pixel Drift Racers: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Pixel Drift Racers is a drifting game. We keep data collection to the minimum the game needs to work. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (cars, parts, cash, settings and best scores) is saved only on your own device. We never receive it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Online play

When you choose to play online, your device connects directly to other players' devices to share your car, position and the driver name you pick. A connection service (PeerJS) helps devices find each other. If you host a room, the room code, your driver name, the map, the weather and the player count are put on a public server list. Rooms drop off the list within about a minute after they close. We don't keep this information after that. Your IP address is visible to the players you connect to and to the connection service, as is normal for online games.

Purchases

Cash Packs are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handle the payment. We never see your name, card details or account. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase happened, so the game can add the cash.

Children

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Driver names are chosen by the player, so please don't use your real name.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
