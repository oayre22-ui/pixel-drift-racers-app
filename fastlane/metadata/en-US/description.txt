Pixel Drift Racers is a top-down pixel-art drifting game all about style, smoke and tuning.

DRIFT
- Link long, smoky drifts to build huge combos
- 8 tracks: harbor, mountain touge, city, downtown, sakura, desert and more
- Weather that changes every event: rain, snow, fog, night and storms
- Drift assists for beginners, or switch them all off

BUILD
- 18 cars from cheap starter cars to high-power monsters
- 24 categories of parts: engines, turbos, gearboxes, suspension and more
- Tune your gearbox, suspension and more with the Mechanic
- Body kits, liveries and paint for every car

COMPETE
- Career events: drift, tandem and race
- Online multiplayer: host a room or join friends with a 5-letter code

Optional Cash Packs are available to speed things up, but everything can be earned by racing.
